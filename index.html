            Meanwhile, upstairs

            The chairman left before the manager did.

            Todd Boehly sold up on 16 September 2026. Clearlake Capital now controls the club, and said nothing would change in the club's day-to-day running, leadership or strategy.

            • Summer in: £340m on 11 signings, led by Morgan Rogers at £117m.
            • Summer out: £383m, including Enzo Fernández to Man City for £125m.
            • Net: roughly £43m profit. The squad got younger again.
            • Europe: none this season. The Carabao Cup was the shortest route to Wembley, and it closed tonight.
